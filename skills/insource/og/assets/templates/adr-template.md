## Choice

1. **Palette** — two roles in one module: `BRAND_MARK` measured from the artwork (never re-toned),
   `BRAND_ACCENT` mirrored from `<stylesheet>` and locked by a test.
2. **Renderer** — `<lib>@<exact version>`, `loadSystemFonts: false`, `fontFiles: [<ttf>]`.
3. **Encoder** — hand-written 8-bit indexed PNG + median cut + Floyd–Steinberg, in `<path>`, exposed as
   `<subpath>` (outside the browser barrel).
4. **Cards** — static per family × locale (committed) in front of a dynamic renderer; degradation to the
   static card on every failure, always HTTP 200.
5. **Copy** — keys only (`<list>`), one resolver shared by the generator and the renderer.
6. **Assets** — generated by `<script>`, committed, with `--check` in `prebuild`.
