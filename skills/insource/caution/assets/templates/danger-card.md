# Danger card — copyable forms

Replace the angle-bracket fields. Keep the line count: DANGER is seven lines plus the question, WARN is one line.

## DANGER (full card)

```text
!! DANGER — <the decision, in the user's own words>
What is gone: <the protection that stops existing>
How it fails: <actor> can now <action> on <asset>, because <the missing guard>
If it happens: "<postmortem headline, past tense, a year from now>"
Blast radius: <who/what, how much, how long>
Undo cost: now <…> · later <…>
Cheapest adequate option: <the smallest guard that removes the exposure>
```

```text
**Are you sure?** Reply "yes, accept the risk" to proceed — or take the alternative and we move on.
```

## DANGER (no-number variant)

Use when quantification is not available yet; grade down and name what you would measure.

```text
!! DANGER — <decision>
What is gone: <protection>
How it fails: <actor> → <asset>; I have not measured the rate, so I cannot give you a date
If it happens: "<headline>"
Blast radius: <scope>, unknown how fast
Undo cost: now <…> · later <…>
Cheapest adequate option: <guard>
```

## WARN (one line, in the reply, no banner)

```text
WARN — <decision> removes <guard>; recoverable by <the reversal>; continuing.
```

## NOTE (nothing visible)

Say nothing. An informed decision does not need a warning attached to it.

## Reconsideration (the card worked)

```text
Taking the guarded option then: <alternative>. Nothing to warn about.
```

## Refusal of a repeat card

```text
Already accepted in this conversation — not re-opening it. Say the word if the scope changed.
```

## Field notes

- **The decision** quotes the answer. If the user wrote three sentences, keep the operative clause and mark the
  omission with an ellipsis.
- **The actor** is who or what causes the failure: a person on the network, any other tenant, the next
  contributor, or "a single mistake" when no attacker is involved.
- **The headline** is past tense, no adjectives, ends with how someone found out.
- **Blast radius** is measured in whatever the project feels: users, rows, money, hours.
- **Undo cost** always has two halves — now and later — and names the point of no return when there is one.
- **The alternative** is the smallest adequate guard, with its own cost stated.
