# Placeholder-style illustrations

**ID:** `shape-assembled-illustration`  
**Catalog section:** Imagery  
**Official modality:** Source

## Definition and empirical evidence

This is a review signal for illustrations assembled from simple geometric shapes that may read as generic or provisional. Geometry is also a deliberate illustration language in mature systems; simple shapes alone do not mean unfinished work.

## Underlying principle and potential harm

Illustration should support the surrounding task/content or an intentional brand role. Art with no clear purpose, a mismatch with the product’s visual language, or placeholder-like execution may distract, weaken coherence, or make a finished experience feel less considered. These are contextual risks, not properties of simple construction.

## Recognition signals

- The subject/idea is unclear at actual display size or inconsistent with adjacent copy.
- Shapes, palette, stroke, proportions, or composition appear uncoordinated relative to approved examples.
- The asset occupies space without clarifying a concept, guiding a task, or serving a defined brand role.
- SVG paths using circles/rectangles/polygons and flat fills reveal construction, not quality or intent.
- If informative, the illustration’s meaning may not be available in nearby text or an appropriate alternative.

## Causes to investigate

Possible causes include a prototype/placeholder retained in a finished screen; a generic asset selected without brand fit; inconsistent use of an illustration system; or deliberate abstraction whose purpose is not apparent outside context.

## Exceptions and false positives

- Geometric illustration can be intentional and systematic; CFPB explicitly builds illustrations from common geometric shapes.
- Low-fidelity shapes may explain UI/workflows in onboarding without overwhelming the explanation.
- Abstract/ambient graphics can be brand texture rather than literal illustration; assess that role separately.
- Decorative imagery may use an empty text alternative; informative art needs an alternative conveying its essential information.
- Sparse style/limited palette does not prove placeholder status; technical polish alone does not prove product fit.

## Correction guidance

Confirm role, audience, brand direction, and whether the asset is finished. If placeholder-like or unhelpful, replace/refine it: clarify the idea, composition, relative size, palette, stroke, and detail. If geometric minimalism is intentional, preserve it and define consistent rules. Add appropriate alternatives for informative illustrations and avoid redundant announcements for decorative art. Review beside real copy at actual size.
