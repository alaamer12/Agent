# Tiny numbered section labels

**ID:** `numbered-section-labels`  
**Catalog section:** Layout & Space  
**Official modality:** Source

## Definition and empirical evidence

This pattern concerns small numbers placed alongside section headings. Numbering is useful when it communicates real order, progress, or cross-reference. Decorative numerals on independent sections can add a cue or imply a sequence that does not exist; the presence of numbers alone proves neither.

## Underlying principle and potential harm

Use visual and semantic structure to help readers understand organization and navigate content. Ordered lists communicate actual sequence/ranking, while headings should reflect the content outline. Unnecessary numbers may compete with titles or create a false sense of order, but can be valuable wayfinding when order is real.

## Recognition signals

- Section numbers appear across independent content without a progression or useful reference role.
- The sequence changes or becomes inconsistent after sections are rearranged.
- A number suggests process order, but the task is nonlinear or users can enter any section.
- Visual numbering substitutes for semantic heading/list structure or is not communicated appropriately where the sequence matters.

## Causes to investigate

Possible causes include a template numbering every section; process-step styling borrowed for unordered content; or stale numbering after content changes.

## Exceptions and false positives

- Multi-step processes can use numbers/progress indicators to communicate order and position.
- Numbering can support editorial, report, print, or cross-reference navigation.
- Small numerals may be an intentional visual identity when they do not mislead.
- A small number may encode real order; a prominent one may still be decorative.

## Correction guidance

Establish what each number means. If it indicates a real sequence, preserve order and encode the relation clearly in content/appropriate semantics. If sections are independent and numbers add no wayfinding/reference value, remove or de-emphasize them. Keep semantic heading levels correct either way; progress indicators supplement, rather than replace, the current section heading.
