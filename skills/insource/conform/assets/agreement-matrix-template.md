## Status Rules

- **Aligned:** every facet satisfies the joint invariant.
- **Scoped non-conflict:** different scopes or layers are explicitly compatible.
- **Open:** intent or authority is missing.
- **Blocked:** resolution depends on an unanswered decision or unavailable evidence.

Point to canonical documents; do not copy their normative rules into this matrix.
