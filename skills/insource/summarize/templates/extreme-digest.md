# Template: Extreme digest (Extreme mode)

```
- **[Term/label]:** [one-line description]
- **[Term/label]:** [one-line description]
- **[Term/label]:** [one-line description]
```

Rules specific to this template:
- First line is always the conclusion, decision, or headline finding — bottom-line-first still applies.
- Every remaining line is must-keep content only (see references/02-selection.md); nothing should-keep or can-cut survives.
- One line per distinct point; no point repeated, no point split across two lines.
- No connective prose between lines ("furthermore," "as a result," "building on this") — this is a list, not a narrative.
- Still passes the faithfulness check in full (references/05-faithfulness-check.md) — a caveat that changes the claim still gets its own line, compressed to a clause, never dropped for brevity.

## Example

Source: a three-paragraph vendor-selection memo (~180 words).

Extreme digest:
```
- **Recommendation:** Vendor C.
- **Why:** only option integrating without custom work.
- **Trade-off accepted:** not cheapest (Vendor A) or best-supported (Vendor B).
- **Caveat:** contract renewal terms still pending legal review.
```

Four lines, no prose, no repetition, nothing must-keep omitted.
