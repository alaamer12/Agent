# Detection: Em-dash overuse

## Catalog route

The official catalog classifies this as a **source** rule. A punctuation count can find passages to review; it cannot establish overuse, quality, or authorship.

## Candidate signals

1. Locate passages with clustered em dashes and retain sentence/paragraph context.
2. Read aloud and determine whether each dash marks a meaningful break, clarification, or emphasis.
3. Check long sentences, multiple asides, and house-style spacing/conventions.
4. Consider alternatives only when they better express the relation between ideas.

## False-positive controls

- Do not enforce a fixed maximum count.
- Do not accuse a writer or infer AI generation from dash frequency.
- Do not replace every dash automatically; punctuation alternatives change emphasis/meaning.

## Test disposition

Source tests may locate dash clusters, with negatives for clear intentional uses. Editorial/context review is necessary to determine if the prose is interrupted or unclear; regex cannot confirm.
