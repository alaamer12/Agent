# Soft spotlight behind content

**ID:** `radial-spotlight-glow`  
**Catalog section:** Color & Contrast  
**Official modality:** Source

## Definition and empirical evidence

The catalog concerns a soft, concentrated light-like gradient behind content. Color and contrast affect attention; the gradient technique is neutral, and a spotlight is useful when its emphasis matches the content’s intended importance.

## Underlying principle and potential harm

Use emphasis to support the intended hierarchy. A glow whose brightest/saturated region sits behind secondary content or away from the focal point may direct attention incorrectly. A spotlight behind text can produce uneven foreground/background contrast; verify the lowest-contrast area in the rendered gradient.

## Recognition signals

- The glow’s center, intensity, size, or falloff emphasizes a secondary region or competes with a key heading/action/image.
- Removing/softening the glow clarifies the intended hierarchy without losing useful meaning.
- Text crosses the gradient’s varied tones and the lowest-contrast area is difficult to read.
- `radial-gradient()` is a source clue, but the same appearance may come from image editing or another rendering technique.

## Causes to investigate

Possible causes include a reusable hero effect applied by default; a glow added to manufacture emphasis without reviewing content hierarchy; or a promotional lighting treatment carried into a context with a different focal point.

## Exceptions and false positives

- A spotlight may highlight a product/image or key content when it supports the task.
- A restrained glow can be intentional brand or atmospheric lighting.
- Similar visual effects may be baked into an image rather than generated by CSS; implementation method does not decide quality.
- A subtle glow may add depth without competing. Gradient presence alone does not establish misdirected attention or poor readability.

## Correction guidance

Identify the intended focal point. If the glow emphasizes the wrong area, remove/reposition it or reduce intensity/spread; then verify the intended hierarchy. Keep intentional brand/product treatments scoped to contexts where they serve a purpose. Recheck text contrast across the entire rendered gradient and review relevant viewports/states.
