# Detection: Calling things “theater”

## Catalog route

The official catalog classifies this as a **source** rule. Phrase matches identify passages for review; they cannot establish vagueness, truth, quality, or authorship.

## Candidate signals

1. Find “X theater” constructions and preserve sentence/paragraph context.
2. Check whether the text explains the intended outcome, observable behavior, evidence of a gap, and what is or is not achieved.
3. Distinguish established analytic terms from casual labels and literal performance/theatrical usage.
4. Check that claims are scoped to evidence and separate perceived reassurance from measured effectiveness.

## False-positive controls

- Do not flag a phrase solely because it contains “theater.”
- Do not treat “security theater”/“compliance theater” as inherently empty; inspect supporting argument.
- Do not assume visible process is useless or infer AI authorship.

## Test disposition

Source tests can locate terminology; negative fixtures should include evidence-backed analytic use and literal theater context. Editorial review assesses specificity and substantiation; regex cannot confirm.
