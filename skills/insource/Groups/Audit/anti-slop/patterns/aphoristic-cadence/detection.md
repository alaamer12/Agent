# Detection: Forced contrast

## Catalog route

The official catalog classifies this as a **source** rule. Repeated phrase frames can locate candidates; context determines whether the contrast is meaningful, redundant, or misleading.

## Candidate signals

1. Search for repeated negative-positive frames such as “not X, but Y.”
2. Read each pair in its paragraph: does the second clause add a distinct, accurate point?
3. Check recurrence within the passage/document and whether contrast clarifies a genuine distinction or implies a false binary.
4. Preserve the surrounding text and genre before assessing style.

## False-positive controls

- Do not ban the construction or enforce a maximum count.
- Do not flag a single useful correction/contrast solely by pattern.
- Do not infer AI authorship from phrase frequency or this rhetoric.

## Test disposition

Source tests can locate repeated contrast frames, with negative cases for meaningful antithesis and concise misconception correction. Editorial review determines usefulness/redundancy; regex cannot confirm.
