# Transparency Report

Numbers on what you removed, handed over, and enforced — the trust artifact for products that host
or relay other people's content. Gate: **not a startup default.** EU hosting providers owe an
annual DSA Art. 15 report once they outgrow the micro/small exemption (<10 staff and ≤€2M turnover
keeps Art. 15 off the table for ordinary hosting); VLOPs report semi-annually (Art. 42). Below the
line, the honest alternative is a short "How we handle requests" page — or nothing. Copying a
Google-shaped template with fields you cannot source is worse than silence.

## Must include

1. **Period + covered services**, definitions matching the counts (a metric without a definition
   is a claim).
2. **Counts tables** — government orders by country and type, notices received/actioned/rejected,
   own-initiative moderation, complaints and appeals with reversal rates; **zero rows stay
   visible** (omitting them reads as hiding); automated vs human split with an accuracy/error
   note; median response times.
3. **Methodology and known gaps**, stated; machine-readable download (CSV/JSON) beside the prose.
4. **DSA mechanics where applicable** — statements of reasons flow into the Commission's public
   Transparency Database (Art. 24(5), via API, no personal data); IR (EU) 2024/2835 fixes the
   templates and harmonized periods (first full cycle 2026); publishing the report but skipping
   the database feed is the classic partial-compliance failure.
5. **Archive** of prior reports (year-over-year is the point; redefining metrics between years is
   the cheat).

## Ask the user - never invent

Every number (pulled from real moderation/routing logs), covered services, whether DSA applies at
all. A transparency report's entire value is that each figure traces to a log.

## Failure patterns

Prose without numbers; importing big-tech field sets the pipeline cannot fill; PDF-only; no
objection/complaint routes cross-linked from `takedown.md`; a voluntary report implying a duty
that does not exist yet (scale honesty).
