# About

Who is behind the product, in verifiable terms. The legal notice answers "which entity"; this
answers "which humans, why, since when". Thin and honest beats rich and fabricated - the origin
story is the most invented paragraph on the web, and this skill does not invent it.

## Must include

1. **What the product is, one sentence** - the same substance the README's first line carries
   (`readme-md` rules: purpose, not stack).
2. **Who operates it** - the team or solo builder, named as they want to be named; the operating
   entity links to `legal-notice.md`.
3. **Why it exists** - ONLY what the operator states; an inferred founding story gets the
   clarification question, not a narrative. If nobody knows, the honest line is one sentence about
   what the product does today.
4. **Where it stands** - real, checkable facts with dates where relevant: launch timing, user or
   content scale **only if the operator confirms the number**, funding status if publicly stated.
   A count nobody can verify is decoration; a count that is wrong becomes the quote that sinks the
   page.
5. **How to reach the humans** - `contact.md` link; press/`careers` links if those pages exist.

## Writing professionally

- First-person plural is fine when a real team exists; "we" with one solo operator reads odd -
  the choice is theirs (ASK the voice: the `personalize`/`voice` law applies - the project's voice
  settings govern this page).
- Locales: the About page is the most human document on the site - translation quality matters
  more than word count; same meaning across language versions (`presentation.md` rule).
- No stock photos of hands-and-keyboards; real team or no team imagery - fabricated faces are the
  catalog's worst honesty failure.

## Ask the user - never invent

Founding facts; real numbers; names and how they want to appear; entity relationship (who legally
operates vs who builds); anything "since 2019"-shaped.

## Failure patterns

A generated mission statement nobody wrote; "used by millions" with nothing behind it; an About
listing people who left; contactless About on a product asking for signups (some jurisdictions want
human contact reachable - `contact.md`).
