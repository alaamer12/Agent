# Promotions & Sweepstakes Rules

The Official Rules page — the binding terms of a giveaway, contest, or prize promotion, and the
instrument that keeps prize + chance + consideration from becoming an illegal lottery. Gate: exists
only while a promotion runs (or with an archive of past ones); straight discounts, coupons, gift-
with-purchase, loyalty points need nothing here.

## Must include

1. **Sponsor identity** — legal name + address (and administrator if separate); the promotion name
   identical everywhere it appears.
2. **"No purchase necessary. A purchase will not increase your chances of winning."** + the free
   alternate means of entry (AMOE), with equal prominence — the keystone clause.
3. **Eligibility** — age of majority, permitted regions, employee/agency exclusions, "void where
   prohibited or restricted by law".
4. **Entry window** with dates, times, and **time zone**; every entry route and limits.
5. **Prizes** — components, quantity, approximate retail value each and total (ARV), no-
   substitution/no-cash-option truth or the conversion rule.
6. **Odds** — stated or their basis ("entries received × prizes"), and for instant-win the seeded-
   pieces truth.
7. **Winner mechanics** — selection (drawing date or judging criteria/weights), notification
   method, response window, alternate winners, delivery, forfeiture.
8. **Taxes** — prizes are income; W-9/affidavit; 1099 reporting (US prize-threshold changed to
   $2,000 for tax years beginning after 2025 — state the real obligation, don't template).
9. **Releases and rules of the road** — liability caps, fraud/technical-failure modification,
   governing law/arbitration if intended, platform disclaimer ("not sponsored or endorsed by…"),
   UGC rights grant for photo/video contests.
10. **Free copy / winners list** route (required with NY/FL filings; good practice always).

## Jurisdiction triggers (ASK before assuming)

US filings key on **total prize value**: NY >$5,000 → state registration ~30 days pre-launch +
bond for high values; FL >$5,000 → filing 7 days pre-launch (filed rules may not be edited); RI
>$500; Canada requires the skill-testing question (and French for Quebec; its registry was
abolished 2023); each adds winner-list duties. Below threshold or excluding those states → the
rules page alone carries it.

## Ask the user - never invent

Prize value and count; states/countries entered; entry routes as actually built; judging truth;
who files what; the platform release language.

## Failure patterns

Purchase-linked odds with no AMOE (an illegal lottery in plain sight); missing ARV/odds; editing
filed FL rules; "follow, tag and share to enter" on Meta; recycled arbitration from a template the
sponsor never read; entry form collecting marketing consent silently (link `us-state-privacy.md`
notice-at-collection duty).
