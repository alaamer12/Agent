# Shipping Policy

Physical goods only - the first filter question. A digital product with this page is the skill's
named over-engineering failure; say "no shipping page: no physical delivery" and move on.

## Must include

1. **Methods** - carriers/services the product actually uses (ASK; a carrier claim you cannot
   fulfill is a promise broken on day one).
2. **Lead time vs delivery time - separate, always:** "ships within N business days; then M-N days
   in transit." Combining them into "arrives in ~2 weeks" is the #1 dispute generator.
3. **Zones and costs** - the zone table (country groups, cost, transit), or a pointer to checkout
   pricing with the rule that checkout price is the price.
4. **Processing cutoffs** - weekends, holidays, custom items.
5. **Tracking** - where it appears, delay norms.
6. **Lost/damaged** - who investigates, the window, the replacement promise, carrier-limit honesty.
7. **International** - customs/duties: who pays (the honest two answers are "we prepay" or
   "you pay on arrival" - pick the true one), restricted destinations list.
8. **Returns** - pointer to `refund-policy.md` (one owner: the money rules live there).

## Writing professionally

- The zone table is the page; prose wraps the table, not the reverse.
- Numbers are the product's logistics reality - verified against the operator's own process,
  never taken from a competitor's page.

## Ask the user - never invent

Carriers, lead/transit times per zone, cost model, customs stance, restricted countries, damaged
claim process and window.
