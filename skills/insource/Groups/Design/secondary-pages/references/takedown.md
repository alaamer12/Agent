# Take-down / Abuse reporting

How to make content claims STOP reaching people: copyright (DMCA-shaped or the local equivalent),
and non-copyright abuse (impersonation, harassment, illegal content) - two different paths, both
belong on one page or two clearly linked ones.

## Must include - copyright path

1. **What to send** - the elements a notice legally needs: identification of the work and the
   infringing URL, contact of the claimant, a good-faith statement, an accuracy statement, the
   claimant's signature (name is enough in the form).
2. **The designated agent** - the named contact/address; some registries require filing it with
   the copyright office - ASK whether the project intends the safe-harbor route; if not, say what
   process replaces it.
3. **What happens next** - removal/ disabling timeline (state the real one), notice to the poster.
4. **Counter-notice** - the poster's defense path, what the claimant then does (restore after N
   days absent a court action - the number is a policy choice: ASK).
5. **Repeat infringers** - the stated policy threshold approach ("we terminate after N confirmed"
   or "case by case" - honest beats precise-but-untrue).

## Must include - abuse path

- Report categories (impersonation, harassment, illegal content, spam) each with the route, the
  evidence a reviewer needs, expected response time (ASK the real number), and whether the
  reporter is notified of the outcome.
- The enforcement consequence links to `acceptable-use.md` (one owner).

## Writing professionally

- One report form or email beats five addresses. Machine-parseable where volume is plausible.
- Timeline claims are support's promise: only write what the operator can actually meet.

## Ask the user - never invent

Jurisdiction posture (US safe-harbor vs EU DSA notice mechanisms vs "general good-faith process");
agent contact; response times; counter-notice window; repeat-infringer stance; which abuse
categories the product actually receives.

## Failure patterns

DMCA text pasted for a product hosted nowhere near US law; a takedown page while NO report channel
exists; the abuse list silent on the one category the product is famous for.
