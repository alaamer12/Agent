## 1. Scope & Boundaries

- Owns: the test ladder and its budget, directory and naming contract, fixture lifecycle, generator
  vocabulary and identity rules, test-data ownership, the No-String Rule and its enforcement,
  determinism seams, conformance tests, quarantine policy.
- Does NOT own: compiler/type strictness (`language-architecture.md`), failure payload shapes
  (`error-architecture.md`), schema and constraints (`db-architecture.md`), extension contracts
  (`plugin-architecture.md`), platform seams (`cross-platform-architecture.md`). This document
  consumes them and makes them assertable.
