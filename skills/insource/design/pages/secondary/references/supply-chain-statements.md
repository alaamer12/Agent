# Supply-Chain & Corporate Statements

The late-scale family: modern slavery statements, supplier codes of conduct, sustainability
reporting, conflict minerals. Gate first — **none of these belongs to a startup**: they trigger on
size, listing, or being audited by enterprise buyers; premature publication is ESG theatre and
invites greenwashing liability. Each entry point below names its own threshold.

## Modern slavery statement [UK MSA §54 / Australia Commonwealth MSA]

- **Trigger:** UK — body corporate carrying on business in the UK, supplying goods/services,
  turnover ≥ £36M (incl. subsidiaries); Australia — consolidated revenue ≥ A$100M, filed on the
  national register against seven mandatory criteria (2025 consultation on a A$50M cut and
  penalties: not yet law).
- **Must include:** structure and supply chains; policies; due-diligence process; risk assessment;
  effective KPIs; training; the financial year covered; **board approval and a named director's
  signature**; homepage-prominent link; prior statements kept.
- **Mistakes:** "we are committed" boilerplate with no KPI; missing sign-off/date; claiming chains
  are "slavery-free" (a statistic nobody can certify); stale PDF after fiscal rollover.

## Supplier / vendor code of conduct

- **Trigger:** procurement expectation — enterprise buyers audit it, and it is the delivery
  mechanism for the statements above and CSDDD-style due-diligence questionnaires.
- **Must include:** scope/definitions; labour (forced, child, hours, wages, association), H&S,
  environment, ethics (bribery, conflicts, IP, data); minerals; **flow-down to sub-tiers**; audit
  right; non-retaliation + a grievance route; remediation ladder to suspension/termination;
  version + date; an acceptance mechanism.
- **Mistakes:** an unsigned PDF; no consequence clause (a code without teeth); misalignment with
  the framework the buyer uses (RBA Code v8, amfori BSCI/SMETA); no way to report.

## Sustainability / ESG page

- **Trigger:** EU CSRD post-Omnibus I — >1,000 employees **and** >€450M turnover (assured ESRS,
  FY2027+); non-EU groups over the EU-turnover gate (FY2028); US: SEC climate rules effectively
  rescinded-proposed (2026) but California SB 253/261 live (first reports 2026-27). Below those:
  **the honest small-company form is a plain page** — what we measure, the baseline number, what
  we do not know yet (EFRAG VSME is the voluntary standard) — or nothing.
- **Greenwashing is the live legal risk:** Empowering Consumers Directive (from 27 Sep 2026) bans
  generic green claims and uncertified label schemes; "carbon neutral" on offsets is finished
  (PAS 2060 withdrawn end-2025); the Green Claims Directive was withdrawn in progress — the ban
  still lands via the ECD.
- **Mistakes:** leaf-art with zero figures; intensity ratios hiding rising absolutes; marketing
  copy merged into the statutory statement; undated page.

## Conflict minerals [US SEC / EU importers]

- **Trigger:** SEC issuers shipping products needing 3TG where necessary to functionality (Form SD
  annually by 1 June, reasonable country-of-origin inquiry still required — 2024 guidance
  withdrawal removed the FAQ safe-harbour, not the rule); EU Regulation 2021/1247 bites 3TG
  **importers** above volume thresholds. "Conflict-free" labelling stays voluntary — say what the
  inquiry found, not what it did not.
- **Must include:** policy; smelter/garbage-facility list route; CMRT template for suppliers;
  escalation; the honest description of inquiry limits.

## Writing professionally (family-wide)

One fact one owner: supplier code links from Privacy/security pages rather than restating them;
statements cite fiscal years; the board approval line is a date and a name.

## Ask the user - never invent

Turnover vs each threshold; entities covered; the actual due-diligence programme; signed-off KPIs
and baseline figures; which framework buyers demand.

## Failure patterns

A 12-person studio publishing a modern-slavery statement "for completeness"; voluntary ESG copy
reading like CSRD assurance; supplier code with no audit or grievance clause; recycled prior-year
figures with the old date painted over.
