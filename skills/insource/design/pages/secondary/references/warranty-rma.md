# Warranty & RMA

The express promise on physical goods, and the claim path that proves it is real. Exists only when
the project *chooses to warrant* or is the producer/importer of its own-brand hardware — a pure
retailer forwards the manufacturer's warranty and says so; SaaS publishes an SLA, not a warranty
(never fake this document).

## Must include

1. **What is promised** — scope, duration, start event (purchase vs delivery), what "lifetime"
   means (undefined "lifetime" is a claim, not a term), who is covered (original purchaser?
   transferees?).
2. **US: the MMWA disclosure format, if you publish a written warranty on consumer products >$10**
   — one clear document with: covered parts, exclusions, what the warrantor will do and pay for,
   term/duration, **step-by-step claim procedure with warrantor address and a toll-free line**,
   dispute mechanism (informal settlement or warrantor's choice), implied-warranty limits followed
   by the mandated "Some States do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or
   consequential damages…" sentence, and the "specific legal rights / other rights vary by State"
   line. Writing a warranty **forbids** disclaiming implied warranties; tie-in clauses (authorized
   service required) are banned.
3. **EU: the statutory floor, stated not mistaken for the promise** — Directive (EU) 2019/771:
   seller liable 2 years from delivery, conformity presumed the first year, staged remedies
   (repair/replacement → price reduction/rescission); from 27 Sep 2026 the **harmonised legal-
   guarantee notice** is mandatory in every online selling interface, and a free whole-product
   guarantee beyond 2 years carries the **GARAN label** (Reg. (EU) 2025/1960).
4. **The RMA flow** — how to open a claim (working route, not an inbox wish), RMA number, who
   pays shipping each leg, inspection turnaround (real numbers), remedy ladder, loaner/replacement
   condition, packaging.
5. **What a warranty is NOT** — retailer returns/refunds live in `refund-policy.md`; extended
   "warranties" are service contracts (insurance filings, state law) and get their own document —
   conflating them is the failure.

## Writing professionally

- The page box and the printed card use identical wording; divergence between them is the classic
  16 CFR 702 trap.
- Show the warranty text near the product description (US online sellers: 16 CFR 702 — display it
  or state how to get it pre-purchase).

## Ask the user - never invent

Whether they warrant at all; term and what it covers; real RMA steps and addresses; who the
producer vs importer is (EU conformity duties); jurisdiction set; extended-warranty products.

## Failure patterns

Retail returns page titled "Warranty"; "lifetime" undefined; registration-card-as-condition making
a "full" warranty limited; box wording ≠ page wording; US warranty text missing the two mandated
state-law sentences; SaaS copying a warranty page when they mean an uptime SLA.
